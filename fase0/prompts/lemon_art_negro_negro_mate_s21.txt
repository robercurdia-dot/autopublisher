CORRECTIONS — learned from what we have already published:
  LABELS: at this size printed labels read as texture. Get the colour, the layout and the emblem right and leave the small print unreadable. Do NOT spell out brand names letter by letter: a wrong word is worse than a blur.
  OPENING: the first shot lasts 1.5 seconds at most, and the first support must be on screen before second 2. Never open with the product alone in a hand.

END STATE, non negotiable: product seated on the support and LIT, glowing warm and lighting the objects around it.

CORRECTIONS — learned from what we have already published:
  OPENING: the first shot lasts 1.5 seconds at most and opens on the situation the viewer already recognises as their own — the scene as it is BEFORE what we sell exists in it. What is being sold is NOT in frame yet. Never open by presenting it.
  Real-world branded objects are identified by their PHYSICAL identity and never by their printed text: silhouette, proportions, material, colour, and non-text marks like an emblem, a seal or a coloured band. Any printed text in frame — on the product, on the props, on the set — stays small, turned away or out of focus. Never make printed text the subject of a shot.
  PAYOFF: the end state — the thing this piece exists to demonstrate — must be on screen before second 3. If the shot list puts it later, compress what comes before it: one complete cycle through to the result first, variations after. Showing the result late is paying for reach that leaves before seeing it.
  FRAMING: always show the COMPLETE combined object — what is being sold together with whatever it acts upon — as one single object. Never crop to the product on its own. What is being sold is the change, and the change is only visible when both parts are in frame.
  FRAMING: keep the upper third of the frame clear. A caption is burned there in post, and if the subject occupies that band the caption covers it or becomes unreadable.
  ARC: start in the state where the product adds nothing and end in the state where its value is obvious. If what is being sold only shows in particular conditions, the clip must travel to them — shooting entirely in the favourable state cancels the demo.
  La duracion no se hereda de la referencia ni se deja en el valor por defecto: se fija en la que usan las piezas de mas alcance del nicho, medida en el ultimo barrido. Quedarse en la mediana del monton es quedarse con los resultados del monton.

END STATE, non negotiable: product seated on the support and LIT, glowing warm and lighting the objects around it.

NEGATIVE: no text overlay, no captions, no subtitles on screen; no glossy finish on the product; no size change between product and support; no aspect ratio change.
